        Built by Akshay Bhatkhande · Coded with AI (Cursor). Product direction, requirements, and portfolio workflow by Akshay; implementation assisted by AI.

        Educational portfolio-construction and research tooling for a long-horizon Roth — not a licensed financial advisor and not personalized investment advice. Market Outlook rows are probabilistic direction + range estimates with a stated confidence level, built from public end-of-day data, news sentiment, and SEC-derived fundamentals — not a guaranteed next-day price; no source, free or paid, can promise one. Recommendations are limited to buying/selling/trimming stocks, ETFs, and mutual funds — never options, calls, puts, or any derivatives strategy. Share counts are whole shares only (no fractionals). Mutual fund orders execute once daily at the next-calculated NAV, not intraday. In a cash IRA, proceeds from a same-day sell may not have settled yet to fund a same-day buy (T+1). Review-to-review history is saved only in this browser's local storage and is never sent anywhere.
